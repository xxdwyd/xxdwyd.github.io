# 🪪 About me
Wu Yundong, male, Doctorate in Education. I serve as an expert for the "Science and Technology Innovation China" program of [the China Association for Science and Technology](https://www.kczg.org.cn/xuezhe/details?id=509038), a startup mentor for the "Yangfan Plan" of the Fujian Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, an expert in [Public services for small and medium-sized enterprises in Jiangsu Province](https://smejs.cn/expert_show.aspx?applyid=216748), an expert for public services for small and medium-sized enterprises in Jiangsu Province, a council member of the Art Research Committee of the Asian Business Research Institute, an entrepreneurship mentor at Quanzhou Entrepreneurship Service Group, and a specially appointed expert at the Huai’an Institute of Career Planning and Design. Additionally, I have been granted 5 national design patents, 3 national utility model patents, and 24 software copyrights, including one for the "Startup and Innovation Plan Industry Survey and Analysis System," and have completed 5 research projects（4 completed projects and 1 ongoing project.）.

Research Interests: 
- Educational Psychology
- Innovation and Entrepreneurship Education
- Vocational Education
